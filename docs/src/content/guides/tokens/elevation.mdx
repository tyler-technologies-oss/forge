---
title: Elevation Tokens
description: Tokens that define the shadow and depth of elements.
---

Elevation tokens define the shadow and depth of elements.

Each token corresponds to a specific elevation level at a specific height on the z-axis. The higher the elevation level, the higher the element appears to be on the z-axis.
