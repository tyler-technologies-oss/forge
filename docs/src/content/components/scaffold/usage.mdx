## Usage

`forge-scaffold` positions content through named slots — `header`, `body`, `footer`, `left`, `right`, and `body`-prefixed variants for a header/footer/left/right scoped to just the body — plus `viewport` to size itself to the full viewport height. Use it for a full page (typically <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}components/app-bar/usage/`}>app bar</a> in `header`, a <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}components/drawer/usage/`}>drawer</a> in `body-left`, and page content in `body`), or to structure a smaller surface like a <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}components/dialog/usage/`}>dialog</a> or <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}components/card/usage/`}>card</a> with its own header, scrollable body, and footer.

## Structure

Every slot at once shows how the scaffold positions content: `left`/`right` frame everything, `header`/`footer` pin above and below, and the `body`-prefixed slots do the same scoped to just the scrollable body.
