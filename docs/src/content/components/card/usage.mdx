## Usage

`forge-card` groups content and actions about one topic through `raised` and `no-padding`. Reach for a `forge-scaffold` inside it once the card needs a distinct header, scrollable body, and footer. Use a card to organize related information or preview a record that links to the full one; avoid it when the content inside isn't actually related, or when scanning and comparing homogeneous data matters more than grouping.
