## Usage

`forge-meter` shows a scalar value within a `min`/`max` range through `value`, `tickmarks`, and an optional `low`/`high`/`optimum` split that colors the track green, yellow, or red depending on where `value` falls. Reach for a meter when the value is a relatively static data point, like a storage quota or a satisfaction score — for a task's busy or in-progress state, use <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}components/linear-progress/usage/`}>linear progress</a> instead; despite the visual similarity, the two represent fundamentally different things.

## Segmented

Set `low` and `high` to divide the track into three regions — below `low`, between `low` and `high`, and above `high` — colored green, yellow, or red depending on which region `optimum` falls in relative to `value`. `low` must be greater than or equal to `min` and less than `high`; `high` must be less than or equal to `max` and greater than `low`.

## Grouped

Wrap same-range meters in `forge-meter-group` to lay them out together on a single shared track — useful for parts of a whole. Grouped meters can't be segmented, and every meter in the group must share the same `min`/`max`. Pair a grouped meter with a <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}components/key/usage/`}>key</a> labeling each meter's color and, optionally, its value.
