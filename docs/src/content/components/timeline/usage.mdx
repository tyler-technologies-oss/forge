## Usage

`forge-timeline` connects a series of `forge-timeline-item` children with a vertical line through their markers, each item pairing a `marker` slot with `detail` content and a `theme`-colored indicator. Use `forge-timeline-break` between items to visually separate non-sequential groups. Reach for a plain <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}components/list/usage/`}>list</a> instead when order and time aren't meaningful to the content.

## Basic usage

Each `forge-timeline-item` shows a default circular marker unless something is slotted into `marker`, and its main content goes in the default slot.

<Example description="A timeline of items connected by a vertical line through their markers.">
  <forge-timeline>
    <forge-timeline-item>
      <div slot="detail">Application submitted</div>
    </forge-timeline-item>
    <forge-timeline-item theme="success">
      <div slot="detail">Application approved</div>
    </forge-timeline-item>
  </forge-timeline>
</Example>

## Markers

Slot an icon or other content into `marker` to replace an item's default circular indicator — treat it as decorative, since the item's own content should carry the actual meaning.

<Example description="A timeline item with an icon in its marker slot.">
  <forge-timeline>
    <forge-timeline-item>
      <forge-icon slot="marker" name="check_circle" external aria-hidden="true"></forge-icon>
      <div slot="detail">Step complete</div>
    </forge-timeline-item>
  </forge-timeline>
</Example>

## Timestamps

Place a <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}components/timestamp/usage/`}>timestamp</a> as a direct child of the timeline to show it as a heading alongside the items it applies to, or inside a timeline item to render it as body text.

<Example description="A timestamp heading grouping the timeline items below it.">
  <forge-timeline>
    <forge-timestamp datetime="2024-03-15" format="MMMM dd, yyyy"></forge-timestamp>
    <forge-timeline-item>
      <div slot="detail">Event on this date</div>
    </forge-timeline-item>
  </forge-timeline>
</Example>

## Breaks

Add a `forge-timeline-break` between items to show a gap, separating non-sequential groups of events.

<Example description="A break between two groups of timeline items.">
  <forge-timeline>
    <forge-timeline-item>
      <div slot="detail">Earlier event</div>
    </forge-timeline-item>
    <forge-timeline-break></forge-timeline-break>
    <forge-timeline-item>
      <div slot="detail">Later event</div>
    </forge-timeline-item>
  </forge-timeline>
</Example>

## Sidebar lines

By default, an item draws its connecting line above and below its marker, automatically omitting whichever side doesn't apply to the first or last item. Set an item's `sidebar` to `start`, `end`, `both`, or `none` to control this manually — useful for implying items exist before or after what's actually rendered.

<Example description="An item with only its start sidebar line drawn, implying earlier items exist above it.">
  <forge-timeline>
    <forge-timeline-item sidebar="start">
      <div slot="detail">Implies earlier items above</div>
    </forge-timeline-item>
  </forge-timeline>
</Example>
