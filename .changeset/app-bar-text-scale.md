App bar text now uses Forge's text scale instead of white alphas: the default app bar uses the dark theme's high, medium, and low text in both modes, which in dark mode matches the regular text tokens, and the white app bar uses the light theme's medium text. Inactive tabs use the medium text step. App bar outlines are lighter: fields rest at white a6 and hover at a7, one step above rest, and `outline-low` dividers use a5.
