Added an optional `forge-ai.css` stylesheet that themes components with an AI gradient when the `forge-ai` class is applied. It styles field components (`forge-text-field`, `forge-select`, `forge-chip-field`, and the date, time, and autocomplete components) with a gradient outline and background, and `forge-checkbox`, `forge-radio` (or `forge-radio-group`), and `forge-switch` with a gradient selected state and a glow, using colors that meet 3:1 contrast against `--forge-theme-surface`. It is available from the package at `@tylertech/forge/dist/forge-ai.css` and from the CDN.
