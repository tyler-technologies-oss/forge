A status message that comes from the app rather than the agent, such as "Switched to Research Assistant" or a session-expired notice. Use it in a conversation for notices that are not part of the agent's reply.

Set `content`, and choose a `kind`. `text` renders plain system-message text, and `info`, `warning`, `error` and `success` render an inline-message banner with a matching icon. An optional `header` titles the banner, and `actions` adds buttons (`{ id, label, onClick }`).
