The AI sparkle icon, optionally surrounded by the AI gradient border. Use it to mark AI-generated content or AI features.

<Canvas of={AiIconStories.Demo} />

## Usage

```html
<forge-ai-icon></forge-ai-icon> <forge-ai-icon outline></forge-ai-icon>
```

The icon uses the theme's tertiary color by default. Override it with `--forge-ai-icon-color`.
