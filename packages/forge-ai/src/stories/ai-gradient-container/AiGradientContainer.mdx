A container that wraps its content in the signature AI gradient border. Use it to give AI-related surfaces a consistent look.

<Canvas of={AiGradientContainerStories.Demo} />

## Usage

Choose the border intensity with `variant`: `low`, `medium` (default), `high` or `disabled`. Use `--forge-ai-gradient-container-radius` to change the corner radius.

```html
<forge-ai-gradient-container variant="high">
  <div>Content</div>
</forge-ai-gradient-container>
```
