A short label that appears next to an element on hover or focus. Use it to name icon-only buttons or add a brief hint. It is built on [`forge-ai-overlay`](?path=/docs/components-primitives-overlay--docs) and exported from `@tylertech/forge-ai/core`.

<Canvas of={AiTooltipStories.Demo} />

## Usage

Give the anchor element an `id` and point the tooltip at it with `for`. Set the content with `text`, or slot in markup instead.

```html
<button id="save">Save</button> <forge-ai-tooltip for="save" text="Save your changes"></forge-ai-tooltip>
```

- `placement` is where the tooltip sits relative to the anchor. It defaults to `top`, and the tooltip flips or shifts to stay in view.
- `trigger` is `hover` (default), `focus`, `click` or `manual`. The default also shows on keyboard focus, and Escape closes an open tooltip.
- `delay` (default 500ms) and `hide-delay` (default 0) control how long the tooltip waits before showing and hiding.
- Call `show()`, `hide()` or `toggle()`, or set `open`, to control it from code.
- `forge-ai-tooltip-toggle` fires when the tooltip opens or closes, with `open`, `newState` and `oldState` in its detail.

When closed, the tooltip content is hidden visually but remains available to screen readers.
