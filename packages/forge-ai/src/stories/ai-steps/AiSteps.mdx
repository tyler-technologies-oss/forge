An expandable timeline of the steps an agent took to produce a response. Collapsed, it shows a step count and the first couple of labels. Expanded, it lists every step with a status marker, and a detail card for steps that have a `detail`. Use it to show your work without dumping every intermediate message.

<Canvas of={AiStepsStories.Demo} />

Pass `steps` as `{ label, detail?, status? }` objects. `status` is `pending`, `running`, or `complete` (the default). An empty `steps` array renders nothing.

## Running and complete

While `running` is set, the timeline stays open and the summary is hidden so the user can watch progress.

<Canvas of={AiStepsStories.Running} />

When `running` turns off, the timeline collapses to its summary. An element that first renders with `running` off starts expanded, so a finished run is readable without interaction.

<Canvas of={AiStepsStories.Complete} />

## Steps without detail

Steps with no `detail` render as just a marker and label.

<Canvas of={AiStepsStories.NoDetail} />

## Formatting

`label` and `detail` are rendered as sanitized inline markdown: `` `code` ``, `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~strikethrough~~` and links. Escape literal `*`, `_` and backtick characters with a backslash. Unclosed syntax stays literal while text is still streaming. In the collapsed summary, links appear as plain text, and images or media from other origins render as links.

<Canvas of={AiStepsStories.WithMarkdownLabels} />

## Streaming

Re-assign `steps` as new data arrives. This example adds each step's label first, then its detail once the step completes.

<Canvas of={AiStepsStories.Streaming} />
