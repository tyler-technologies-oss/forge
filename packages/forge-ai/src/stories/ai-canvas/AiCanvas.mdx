A side panel that presents content, such as a document or generated artifact, next to a conversation. It renders a titled header, an actions area, a close button, and a scrollable body. Your app decides when the canvas is shown and how it is laid out.

<Canvas of={AiCanvasStories.Demo} />

## Usage

```html
<forge-ai-canvas title-text="Quarterly summary">
  <p>Panel content goes here.</p>
</forge-ai-canvas>
```

The canvas fills the height of its container. Set `title-text` to give the panel a visible heading and an accessible name. To replace the heading with your own content, use the `title` slot.

### Closing

The close button fires `forge-ai-canvas-close`, which has no detail. The canvas does not hide itself, so remove or hide it in your handler.

### Actions

Add buttons to the `actions` slot to show them before the close button.
